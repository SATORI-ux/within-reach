## Opening Note

```text
Hey Jess.

This was supposed to find you a little differently.

Originally, I had this whole version of it in my head where you would stumble into it without knowing it was coming. Or, realistically, maybe never stumble into it at all, because I made it unnecessarily hidden in a way that felt very reasonable at the time.

Obviously, things changed a little.

You know there was a secret now. You know I was keeping something tucked away in here. But you still do not really know what it was, or what it was meant to hold.

And I think that is why I still wanted to finish it.

Because the point was never just that it was hidden.

The point was that I wanted there to be a place for the things I had not said yet. A place for the memories I keep coming back to. A place for the parts of this that felt too specific, too careful, or too important to just throw into a normal conversation and hope I got the words right.

So this is not exactly the version I first imagined.

But it is still the same feeling.

Still quiet.
Still intentional.
Still full of you.

And maybe, in a weird way, it makes more sense now.

Because you already found the edge of the secret.

This is what was behind it.
```

## Poem: Constantia

```text
Constantia

How early
can someone become
unforgettable?

Before there are names
for what they are?

Before distance
has learned your voice?

Before either of us
knows enough
to be afraid?

I think of you
when I remember
that ceiling light.

The small sound
against the glass.

The old phone glow.

The ridiculous certainty
that somehow,

in a room
with a trapped ladybug,

I had found
someone
I would not forget.

We laughed
about mutant ladybugs

as if the world
had handed us
something small
and strange

just to see
what we would make of it.

That is how you stayed.

Not loudly.
Not perfectly.
Not in a way
I always knew
how to hold.

But there.

In the small,
ordinary moments
that should have passed
without meaning.

In the pause
after a joke.

In the kind of quiet
most people
would never think
to keep.

You made ordinary things
feel marked.

Like some part of you
had touched them
and left them warmer.

I have tried
to call it memory.

I have tried
to call it timing.

I have tried
to call it
the kind of feeling
people survive
and eventually
set down.

But you were never
something I set down.

You were someone
I kept returning to
without understanding
that returning
was already
a kind of truth.

There were years
between us.

I will not pretend
they were nothing.

Time has a way
of teaching people
how to live
around an absence.

It makes rooms
out of missing.

It makes habits
out of silence.

It lets two people
become older
without becoming
less connected.

And maybe
that is what we learned.

Not that distance
means less.

Not that silence
means gone.

But that some feelings
do not disappear
just because neither person
knows what to call them yet.

You were the proof.

The thought
behind time passing,

before I had
a name for it.

The warmth
beneath the years.

The familiar thread
I kept finding
in my hands

even when I thought
I had let go.

I made this place
because I did not know
where else
to put all of this.

Not because
you were small enough
to fit inside it,

but because
what I felt for you
needed somewhere quiet
to exist

before I was ready
to say it plainly.

A little door.

A hidden note.

A soft place
for all the things
I could not trust
myself to say
without losing
the shape of them.

But the truth is,

you were never
the secret.

You were the constant.

The part
that survived
the distance.

The part
that made the past
feel unfinished.

The part
that made the future
feel possible
without asking it
to promise me anything.

And now
when I think of love,

I do not think
of being saved.

I do not think
of being made whole
by someone else’s hands.

I think of choosing
to become someone
who can stay.

Someone who can meet you
without disappearing.

Someone who understands
that being here
is not a feeling.

It is a practice.

A return.

A decision
made again
and again.

You have always been
more than beautiful.

More than funny.

More than soft.

More than the strange,
bright memory
that found me
through a ceiling light
and somehow
never really left.

You are the person
I have measured
so many quiet things against

without knowing
I was doing it.

The voice
that made the dark
less absolute.

The memory
that kept its warmth.

The place
I kept finding
my way back to.

So if this began
as something hidden,

let it end
as something honest.

I have loved you
through unfinished things.

Through silence.

Through distance.

Through versions of myself
that did not yet know
how to stay close
without hiding
from what close meant.

But I know
what I am saying now.

You are not
the person I found
at the end
of this secret.

You are the reason
there was ever
a secret
to find.

Constantia.

Not because nothing changed.

Everything did.

But because
after all of it,

after the years,
the distance,
the silence,
the almosts,
the returning,

there was still
one thing
I recognized.

You.
```

## The Two Names

```text
The Two Names

Constantia

Not just constancy.

Steadiness.
Endurance.
The part that remains recognizable even after everything changes.

The thread that does not disappear just because life moves around it.

That was yours.

Solacium

Not just comfort.

Refuge.
Warmth.
The quiet relief of having somewhere to return to.

The feeling of being met softly by something that still remembers you.

That was mine.

Together, I think they became the closest thing we had to naming this.

The thread,
and the shelter it made.
```
